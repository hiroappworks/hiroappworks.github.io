Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about Consignment Sales Tracker.

Q.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the same Apple Account you used to purchase Pro. In Settings, open “Upgrade to Pro” and select “Restore purchases”. If Pro is already active, there is no need to restore it again. Contact us if it still does not appear.

Q.

Where is my data stored?

Your products, sales locations, inventory, sales and settlements are stored on your device. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

Q.

How do I create a backup?

Select “Create backup” in Settings, then use the iOS share sheet to save the file to Files or another destination. Make sure the file has finished saving before closing the sheet.

Q.

How do I move my data to a new device?

Create a manual backup in Settings and save it to Files or another destination. On your new device, select “Restore from backup” and choose that file. Review the contents and the warning that restoring replaces the app’s current data and photos before confirming. The app does not provide automatic cloud sync.

Q.

I purchased Pro, but it is not active

Try “Restore purchases” from “Upgrade to Pro” in Settings. If the issue continues, describe the purchase issue in our contact form.
