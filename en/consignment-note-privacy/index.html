01

Introduction

Hiro App Works (hereinafter “we”) provides the iOS app “Consignment Sales Tracker” (hereinafter “the App”). This policy explains how user information is handled in the App.

The App is used to manage consignment-sale products, inventory, sales, and settlements on an iPhone. Account registration and login are not required, and the App has no screens for entering a name, address, telephone number, email address, or similar information in order to use it. However, users may voluntarily enter text such as product names, notes, and sales location names.

02

Information handled in the app

Business data entered or created in the App is stored, in principle, on your device. The main contents are as follows.

  • Product information: Product name, optional management number / product number, sale price, initial at-home inventory, notes, registration date and time, discontinued status, and information about where product photos are stored
  • Sales location information: Sales location name, fee method, rate, and amount, notes, and location-use end status
  • Inventory and sales history: Details of deliveries, items brought home, sales, inventory adjustments, and cancellations; quantities, record dates, prices at the time of sale, and notes
  • Settlement information: Applicable period, sales, fees, transfer fees, expected deposit amount, actual deposit amount, difference, settlement status, and itemized details by product
  • Settings and operation history: Settings for automatic suggestions and recording methods, and operation history relating to products, sales locations, and settlements

The App does not have a process for saving business data managed on its screens, such as product names, prices, inventory counts, sales, sales location names, notes, and photos, to our own servers.

03

Information sent to external services

The App communicates with external services to check purchases, understand app usage, and collect optional survey responses. It does not store your business records on our own servers or provide general cloud syncing.

Purchase management (RevenueCat)

We use a purchase management service and Apple’s payment system to handle Pro purchases, restore purchases, and check access to Pro. They process purchase information, service-generated identifiers, and technical information about the app and device.

The App does not send product names, stock records, sales location names, photos, or the contents of CSV files and backups to this service. It also does not provide its own account ID, your name, or your email address. For details, see the purchase management service’s privacy policy.

App usage and optional surveys (version 1.1.3 and later)

We use a third-party app analytics service to help make the App easier to use. We review app launches and feature usage to improve the experience and decide what to develop next.

This includes use of product and sales location registration, deliveries, remaining quantities, sales records, stock adjustments, settlements, the guide, photo changes, CSV files, backups, Pro offers, and purchase or restore actions. We also send the time of the action and limited category and technical information, such as the app’s display language and app and operating system versions.

We do not send product names, item codes, sales location names, quantities, prices, sales amounts, fees, settlement amounts, photos, file names or storage paths, notes, or internal business record IDs. Customer IDs from the purchase management service, purchase transaction IDs, and raw error messages are not sent for app usage analytics either.

To understand continued use of the same installation, we store an identifier on your device and send it with usage information. It is not based on your name or email address. It links usage history within the same installation and remains through ordinary restarts and updates, including when sharing is turned OFF and back ON.

Turning usage sharing off

Sharing is enabled by default. You can turn “Share Usage Data” OFF at the bottom of Settings at any time. This choice stays in place after restarts and updates and stops new usage logs from being sent. Turning it off does not change normal features or your access to Pro. Turning it back on does not resend old unsent logs from before or during the time sharing was off.

Optional surveys

We may offer multiple-choice surveys to help improve the App. Taking part is optional, and choosing not to answer does not limit any features. When you press Submit, we send your selected answers, display language, and information that identifies the survey and response to the analytics service. Surveys do not collect free-text answers or photos. The App also communicates with the service to check which surveys are available.

Responses use a separate identifier and are not directly linked to the identifier used for ongoing usage logs. You can choose to send a survey response even when usage sharing is OFF. Answered or skipped status and response acceptance information are saved on your device.

If a delivery result cannot be confirmed, the App blocks resending to prevent duplicates. You can end that response without resending it. This does not confirm delivery or delete an answer at the receiving service. You cannot answer the same survey again after ending it, but other surveys remain available.

We do not record your screen, automatically collect taps, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. For the receiving service’s information, see the analytics service’s privacy policy.

04

In-app purchases

The App’s Pro features are based on non-consumable in-app purchases through the iOS App Store. Apple processes the payment, and RevenueCat assists with checking the purchase status and Pro entitlements in the App. The App does not have a process for obtaining or storing payment-method information such as credit card numbers entered on Apple’s payment screen.

The App does not save purchase status, the RevenueCat customer ID, or analytics event history in the App’s SQLite data or manual backups. If a purchase or restore fails or is cancelled, the App does not treat it as successful.

05

Analytics information

The App’s Analytics screen summarizes sales records stored on your device. It does not send the underlying sales amounts, quantities, sales rates, expected payments, rankings, or other business records to the third-party app analytics service.

Product registration, stock, sales, settlements, and purchases remain available when usage sharing is OFF or the analytics service cannot be reached.

06

Product photos

You can add product photos from your photo library or camera. The App resizes and compresses them and stores them on your device.

Photos are not sent to purchase management or app analytics services or to our servers. Deleting a photo in the App also deletes the photo file it manages. Copies saved elsewhere through backup or sharing may remain at those destinations.

07

CSV files

For bulk product registration, the App reads a CSV file you choose on your device. Product information and initial at-home stock are saved only after you review the contents and confirm registration. The App does not upload CSV contents to an external server.

UTF-8 files are supported, and files larger than 10 MiB are not read. Temporary copies made for import are removed after processing. Exporting a template opens the iOS share sheet and sends the file to the destination you choose. Destinations such as Files or iCloud Drive apply their own settings and policies.

08

Backup and restore

Backups are manual: you create them from Settings. A backup combines products, sales locations, stock and sales history, settlements, settings, operation history, and product photos in one file.

The file is temporarily placed in the App, then passed to a destination you choose through the iOS share sheet. It is not automatically uploaded to our servers or iCloud. If you choose Files, iCloud Drive, email, or another service, the file is sent there.

When restoring, the App checks the backup you selected and replaces existing data and photos only after your confirmation. Purchase status and app analytics status are not included in backups. Exported backups are not deleted by the App, so remove them from their destination when you no longer need them.

09

Provision to third parties

The App has no feature that provides your business records to third parties for advertising or sale. Usage information and responses are not used for third-party advertising tracking.

External services receive information needed for the purchase management, analytics, optional surveys, and App Store payments described above. Files also go to destinations you choose when sharing. Apart from service providers needed to operate these services and disclosures required by law, we do not independently share information for other purposes.

10

Data management

Your product, stock, sales, and settlement records stay on your device. We have no way to view them directly during normal use of the App. There is no account-based cloud syncing or automatic request to have us delete data.

To keep records consistent, products, sales locations, and transactions with history are normally kept with an ended or cancelled status rather than deleted. You can delete product photos from the editing screen. When you delete the App, local data is handled by iOS. CSV files and backups you exported must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.

Retention of analytics and responses

Usage logs and survey responses from version 1.1.3 and later are sent to and stored in the analytics service’s U.S. cloud environment. The current standard period for searching and analyzing these records is seven years. This period does not establish when every copy, including communications and security records and backups, is erased.

Our production environment is configured to discard source IP addresses from stored analytics events. Location enrichment is also disabled. This setting does not remove all handling of connection information needed for communications and security.

In production, the analytics service’s external AI features and the setting allowing data to be used for internal AI training are disabled. Service providers may process information as needed to operate the service. Retention and deletion of purchase-related information also follow the purchase management service’s and Apple’s policies.

Questions and deletion requests

Please use the contact details below for questions or requests to delete information already sent. Usage logs use an identifier that is not based on your name or email address, so we may be unable to identify the relevant records from your inquiry alone. We respond based on the information we can verify and applicable law.

Updating the App, turning sharing OFF, or deleting the App does not automatically delete information already sent. We cannot promise that every anonymous record can be individually identified and deleted.

11

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this policy in response to changes in the App’s features, the external services it uses, applicable laws, or operations. If there are important changes, we will notify users in a way that can be recognized on this page or in the App. The revised policy applies from the time it is posted on this page.
