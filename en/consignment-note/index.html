Everyday essentials

Start simply. Grow at your own pace.

English and Japanese

Version 1.1 supports both languages. A guide on Home helps you add your first product and sales location, then record a delivery.

Choose your currency

Start a new set of records in JPY, USD, GBP, CAD or AUD. One currency is used for all records and is fixed once you save business data. Records created before v1.1 stay in JPY; the app does not convert currencies.

Free, with an optional Pro upgrade

Free includes up to 10 active products and one active sales location. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes these limits and adds sales analytics and rankings. Both versions are ad-free. See the current price in the app.

CSV and manual backups

Import products from CSV, create backups including product photos, and restore a saved backup in either Free or Pro. Save backups through the iOS share sheet. There is no automatic cloud sync.
