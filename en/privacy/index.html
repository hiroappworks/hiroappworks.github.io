Hiro App Works Privacy Policy

This page explains how Hiro App Works handles information in its general support and contact services.

This includes the general support page, the contact form on this site, and contact by email. Consignment Note is currently available in Japanese only. If you use the app, please see the Consignment Note Privacy Policy for information about how data is handled within the app.

Scope of this policy

This policy covers information users voluntarily send for support or contact. It does not cover data handled by the apps themselves.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the following general support and contact services.

  • Hiro App Works general support page
  • Contact form on this site
  • Contact by email

Each app’s individual privacy policy applies to data handled by the app itself. For the current app covered by this site, Consignment Note, please see the Consignment Note Privacy Policy.

2. Information that may be collected when contacting us

When users contact support, they may voluntarily enter and send the following information for the purpose of their inquiry.

  • Reply email address
  • Message
  • App concerned
  • App version
  • iOS version
  • iPhone model
  • Other information voluntarily included in the message

This is information that users voluntarily send for the purpose of contacting us.

3. Purpose of use

Collected information is used for the following purposes.

  • Responding to inquiries
  • Checking reported issues
  • Providing support
  • Necessary communication

4. Contact form

The contact form is provided on the Hiro App Works website. When a message is sent, the submitted information is registered in the existing Google Form through Google Apps Script. Information saved in Google Forms is subject to Google’s services and privacy policies.

Please see the Google Privacy Policy. The contact form is available from the site contact form.

5. Contact by email

Information sent by email is also used to respond to inquiries, check reported issues, provide support, and make necessary contact.

Contact email contact@hiroappworks.com

6. Confidential information

Please do not send the following types of information through the contact form or by email.

  • Passwords
  • Apple Account passwords
  • Credit card numbers
  • Other highly confidential information

7. Distinction from the apps

Data handled by each app on a device or sent to external services is separate from information that users voluntarily send through the support form or by email.

For information handling by each app itself, please see that app’s individual privacy policy.

If you use Consignment Note Consignment Note Privacy Policy

8. External services

This support service uses Google Apps Script and Google Forms to process form submissions. Please see the privacy policies and other applicable information provided by those services for how information saved on them is handled.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be changed as necessary. Any updated content will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at the address below.
